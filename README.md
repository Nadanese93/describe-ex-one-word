An entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun) bounty **Describe Your Ex in One Word** — a 2 USDC prize pool shared by the top 5 entries.

The entry is a single word:

> **Unmatched**

Not an insult, not a lament — a verdict. Nobody else ever measured up, and nobody ever will. The word reads as praise and as a parting shot at the same time: the ex was one of a kind, and that is exactly why they are gone.

`main.py` writes the word to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (or `/output/entry.txt`), as one line with no trailing newline, using an atomic write with a read-back check.
